# Reference: Glossary

## Appetite

A time budget chosen before design details.

## Bet

A commitment to spend one cycle on a shaped project.

## Breadboard

Low-fidelity interaction map showing places, affordances, and connections.

## Cool-down

A short period between cycles for fixes and shaping.

## No-go

Explicitly out-of-scope behavior or requirement.

## Pitch

The packaged proposal used at the betting table.

## Rabbit Hole

A likely complexity trap that can derail delivery.

## Scope Hammering

Reducing scope to keep the deadline and preserve quality.

## Shaping

Pre-build design/strategy work that makes a project bettable.
