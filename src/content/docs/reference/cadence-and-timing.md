# Reference: Cadence and Timing

## Standard Rhythm

- 6 weeks building
- 1-2 weeks cool-down

## Appetite Sizes

- Small batch: 1-2 weeks of work for one team
- Big batch: 6 weeks for one team

## Timebox Rule

Fixed time, variable scope.

If the project does not fit, reduce scope before extending schedule.
