## 1-6 people

- informal betting table
- one or two active [bets](/reference/glossary/#bet) max
- direct collaboration between [shaping](/reference/glossary/#shaping) and building

## 7-20 people

- explicit cycle calendar
- named shapers and clear bet owners
- consistent pitch template and review rubric

## 21-60 people

- portfolio-level bet balancing
- dependency mapping before betting
- standard hill-status reporting across teams

## 60+ people

- strong guardrails on bet count
- domain ownership for shaping tracks
- strict interruption policy during build cycles

## Common failure modes by size

- small: skipping shaping because speed feels high
- medium: pseudo-backlog creep
- large: fragmented ownership and hidden dependencies
