## Goal

Complete a full Shape Up cycle with one small team and one meaningful project.

## Prerequisites

- 1 shaper (product/design lead)
- 1 designer
- 1-2 programmers
- A candidate problem from customer feedback

## Step 1: Pick an appetite

Choose one appetite before discussing solutions:

- Small batch: 1-2 weeks
- Big batch: 6 weeks

For a first cycle, pick one big batch project.

## Step 2: Shape the project

Create a pitch with:

- Problem
- [Appetite](/reference/glossary/#appetite)
- Solution outline
- [Rabbit holes](/reference/glossary/#rabbit-hole)
- [No-gos](/reference/glossary/#no-go) (out of scope)

Keep the solution concrete enough to build, but leave room for implementation choices.

## Step 3: Bet at the table

Review candidate [pitches](/reference/glossary/#pitch) and choose only what fits available teams for the cycle.

Rules:

- No carry-over assumptions
- No hidden backlog commitment
- Team gets uninterrupted time

## Step 4: Kick off and hand over responsibility

Assign the pitch to one team. Do not pre-split into manager-owned tasks.

Team responsibilities:

- break work into scopes
- integrate one vertical slice early
- track unknowns openly

## Step 5: Run the cycle

During the 6 weeks:

- protect focus (no random interruptions)
- cut scope when needed, not quality
- track progress by "uphill/downhill" confidence

## Step 6: Cool-down

After six weeks, do 1-2 weeks of [cool-down](/reference/glossary/#cool-down):

- bug fixes
- housekeeping
- [shaping](/reference/glossary/#shaping) next [bets](/reference/glossary/#bet)

## Success check

At cycle end, answer:

- Did we ship in the timebox?
- Did the team stay autonomous?
- Were cuts strategic instead of last-minute panic?
- Is the next cycle ready to start?
