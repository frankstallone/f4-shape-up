---
title: "Explanation: Why Shape Up Avoids Backlogs"
description: "Explanation: Why Shape Up Avoids Backlogs"
---
Traditional backlogs create implicit promises and constant context switching. They also inflate planning overhead by mixing raw ideas, partially shaped work, and active commitments.

Shape Up treats incoming ideas as potential inputs to shaping, not queued obligations. Work enters execution only after it is shaped and explicitly bet.

This keeps teams focused, keeps priorities current, and prevents stale commitments from driving the roadmap.
