title: "Explanation: Why Shape Before Betting"
description: "Explanation: Why Shape Before Betting"
---
Betting on unshaped work converts uncertainty into delivery risk.

[Shaping](/reference/glossary/#shaping) first improves decision quality by making three things explicit:

- what problem is being solved
- how much time it is worth
- where the likely traps are

This makes betting a strategic allocation decision instead of a hopeful estimate.
