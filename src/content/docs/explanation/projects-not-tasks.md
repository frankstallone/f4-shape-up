description: "Explanation: Projects, Not Tasks"
---
Shape Up hands teams projects rather than manager-predefined task lists.

Why this matters:

- teams discover real work as they integrate slices
- local decisions happen closer to implementation reality
- managers focus on boundaries and outcomes, not ticket choreography

Task discovery is expected behavior, not planning failure.

The risk is chaos if boundaries are weak. That is why shaping quality and no-gos are critical.
